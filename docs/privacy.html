3. Website analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4), when enabled and with your permission, to understand how visitors use the site. This includes page views, referring sources, approximate geographic information, device and browser details, session identifiers, engagement and interactions such as outbound link clicks. We use these statistics to understand which articles are useful and improve the site.

Consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR) is the legal basis for optional analytics. The site is configured to load GA4 only after the required analytics permissions have been granted. If the consent system cannot establish permission, analytics remains disabled. Google Signals, user-provided data collection, granular location and device data collection, and advertising personalization using GA4 data are disabled in our property settings. We do not send newsletter email addresses or implement a signed-in User-ID in Analytics.

Google processes the measurement data to provide its services. The configured retention period for user-level and event-level data is 14 months, without resetting the user-data retention period on new activity. Standard aggregated reports are not subject to that setting.

More information: How Google uses information from partner sites, Google Privacy Policy and Analytics data retention.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies and browser storage are used to remember information or choices. Depending on the services enabled and your permissions, the following categories may apply:

Essential and preference storage
Used to remember privacy choices and support necessary security functions. Outside the European consent message, the local analytics preference is stored in browser local storage under gta6-analytics-consent-v2 for 180 days. Google’s consent interface uses its own preference storage and expiry rules. This essential storage records privacy choices rather than measuring visits.
Analytics cookies
GA4 can use first-party cookies such as _ga and _ga_* to distinguish visitors and maintain session information. Our tag sets a lifetime of 180 days without automatic renewal on visits; actual storage may expire earlier due to browser restrictions. The site is configured to remove accessible GA4 cookies when analytics consent is rejected or withdrawn. See GA4 cookie usage.
Advertising cookies and identifiers
When enabled, Google and its partners may use advertising cookies and similar identifiers for permitted purposes. Names and lifetimes vary by provider and settings. See AdSense cookie information and the partner details provided in the consent interface.

You can also restrict or remove cookies using your browser settings. Removing preference storage may cause the privacy message to appear again. Browser settings do not necessarily replace the site-specific opt-out or withdrawal controls.
