Quick verdict

Yes, players have found a way—using the Quick Resume bug on Xbox Series X/S hooked to a 120 Hz display—to break GTA VI’s 30 fps cap. But it’s glitchy, fast‑forwards gameplay, and Rockstar hasn’t made this a real mode.

How folks are doing it (from Reddit reports)

Get that Xbox display set to 60 Hz, launch GTA VI, wait for the six stars to show in the intro, then without quitting the game switch your output to 120 Hz. Launch another title, then jump back via Quick Resume. Suddenly, the 30 fps lock drops. But game speed jumps too—the whole world zips forward like you're on double speed.

It’s all over Reddit (user Meowwie64 and others) and sites like Pure Xbox and GamesRadar are running through it too.

What actually happens

  • Yes, frame rates can climb—sometimes nearing 60 fps in empty scenes. Most of the time it’s patchy, 35–45 fps during normal play.
  • The catch? Simulation ties to FPS. Animations, movement, actions all speed up. It’s not smoother, it’s broken-fast.
  • Every source emphasises it’s not an official mode and is unsafe: expect crashes, bugs, weird behaviour. One even noted switching wrong pushes your framerate to 15 fps.

Experts weigh in

Digital Foundry ran tests: averages hover around 36 fps; not a real 60 fps. And they saw screen tearing, instability, and that the way GTA VI is designed ties the simulation tightly to the frame rate. Rockstar built it for stable 30 fps on current-gen.

Your best move right now

If you're curious, go nuts—but this isn’t a way to play the game properly. Keep it standard until—or if—Rockstar patches in a legit performance mode. Until then, 30 fps it is.

Final thought

It's a neat trick, but not a fix. GTA VI was clearly built around 30 fps on Xbox, and this is a happy accident—not performance mode. Rockstar hasn’t sworn to drop it or turn it real. We wait.

FAQ

Can I use this glitch safely for long play sessions?

Not really. Players report inconsistent frame rates, sped‑up animations, possible crashes and bugs. It’s best as a neat trick, not a way to play.

Is there an official 60 fps mode coming?

Rockstar confirmed at launch the game targets 30 fps on consoles and hasn’t offered any performance mode yet. No sign that the glitch will become legit.
