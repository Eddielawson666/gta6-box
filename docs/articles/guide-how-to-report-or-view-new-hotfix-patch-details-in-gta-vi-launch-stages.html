How to view and report GTA VI hotfix details

Short answer

You can view hotfix or patch details in GTA VI only once Rockstar’s published them—right now that’s just the November 23 launch patch. To report issues, you’ve gotta hit Rockstar Support.

Where to see new patch details

Rockstar dumps patch notes on their own Support site, under “Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Updates (PS5 / XBOX Series X|S)”. That’s the only legit source. The two updates before launch (versions 01.002.000 and 01.002.001) had zero notes. Rockstar said nothing about what they changed—so treat any chatter as rumor unless it’s there.

As of November 24, 2026, the only published update is from November 23. That one fixed crashes, weather getting stuck, autosave issues, phone call bugs, stuck Wanted Level, gym bar, and partner bag handoffs. No new missions or content—just stability stuff.

How to check your version and spot updates

  • On PS5: highlight GTA VI, press Options, choose “Information” to see version. Use “Check for Update” to grab new patches.
  • On Xbox Series X|S: go to My games & apps → Manage game/add-ons → Updates.

Turn on auto‑updates in your console settings so hotfixes drop while you're not watching.

How to report bugs you’re hitting

If a problem isn’t fixed or you discover something new, go straight to Rockstar Support and file a bug. Say what console you’re on, your build or version, describe the issue, toss in screenshots or error codes if you can. That’s the only route that counts.

PS5 users getting system error codes (like CE‑numbers) could also check with PlayStation Support—some of those come from the system, not Rockstar’s game.

Why this matters

Rockstar's notes are the only official word—anything else is just conjecture. You’ve got those pre‑launch patches installed, fine, but until a note appears on Support (or Newswire), you don’t actually know what changed.

This November 23 patch is the first proper hotfix post-launch. Expect more in the same spot—check launch updates on Rockstar Support.

Oh, and for those long sessions, grab a solid headset or controller—makes a difference—get one here.

Wrapping up

Only one hotfix patch note so far, and you’ll find it on Rockstar Support. Watch your game version, report bugs there, and keep an eye out—this is just the beginning.
