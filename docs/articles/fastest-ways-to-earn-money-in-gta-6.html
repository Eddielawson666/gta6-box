Fastest Ways to Earn Money in GTA 6

In GTA 6, players looking to amass a quick fortune can follow several efficient strategies across different stages of the game. Here’s how you can earn GTA$ fast, focusing on confirmed methods based on the game’s chapters.

Early Game (Chapters 1–2)

  • Steal and Sell Cars to Moses: After meeting Moses post-“Girls' Trip”, sell stolen cars (without trackers) for over $1,000 each. Look out for texts from Moses that guide you to valuable targets, reducing search time significantly. [S1]
  • Rob Criminals During Crimes: Interrupt criminals mid-heist to snag rewards ranging from $1,000 to $15,000, offering substantial payouts with minimal prep. [S2]

Mid Game (Chapter 3)

  • Sell Supercars to Randy: By Chapter 3, Randy’s Wish List opens, offering $30,000 or more for high-end vehicles, provided they’re in good condition. [S2]
  • Jewelry Store Robberies: Unlocks in Chapter 3, with payouts between $10,000 and $25,000. Focus on disabling alarms for a smooth escape. [S2]
  • Small Bank and Payday Loan Robberies: These targets become available later in Chapter 3, providing $15,000 or more per hit. [S2]

Late Game (Chapter 4 and Beyond)

  • Large Bank Vault Heists: With the “That's Where the Money Is…” mission in Chapter 4, earn up to $205,000 in a few minutes, although be prepared for intense police chases. [S2]
  • Pilot Pete Deliveries: Engage in timely package drops for $12,000 to $20,000 per delivery in Chapter 4’s additional missions. [S2]

Competitive Activities

Gellhorn Raceway events and jobs such as Hit Kingpin Bowling or Billy’s armored van task offer additional income ranging from $1,800 to $4,800 depending on performance. These competitive jobs air during the early game stages. [S3]

Utilize these strategies to navigate the financial challenges in GTA 6 effectively, while enjoying the various layers of gameplay it offers across its expansive map.

FAQ

What is the best way to earn money in the early game?

Stealing and selling cars to Moses, and interrupting criminals mid-crime are the most efficient early-game money-making methods.

How much can I earn from large bank vault heists in the late game?

Large bank vault heists can earn you up to $205,000, although they come with a high police pursuit risk.

Are jewelry store robberies available from the beginning of the game?

No, jewelry store robberies become available in Chapter 3, offering payouts of $10,000 to $25,000.
