Expert Tips for Improving GTA 6 HDR Issues

To improve HDR performance in GTA 6, start by getting your TV and console settings aligned before adjusting anything in the game. Here's a structured approach to tackle the common HDR issues players face.

Adjusting Your TV Display

First, configure your TV with these settings:

  • Enable Game Mode: This mode disables image processing features that can interfere with HDR. This is a key recommendation from Rockstar Support.
  • Activate HGiG: This setting lets your TV utilize the game’s HDR tone mapping effectively, preventing double processing and enhancing picture accuracy (Rockstar Support).
  • Turn Off Extra Processing: Disable AI enhancements, motion smoothing, dynamic contrast, and other similar settings (Rockstar Support).

Calibrate Your Console

Next, ensure your console is set up correctly:

  • PS5: Go to Settings → Screen and Video → Video Output → Adjust HDR and set HDR to “On When Supported” (GTA6Data).
  • Xbox: Navigate to Settings → General → TV & Display Options → Calibrate HDR for Games and follow the on-screen guide to align your HDR settings (GTA6Data).

In-Game HDR Calibration

Once your TV and console are set, adjust the in-game HDR settings:

  • Open GTA 6’s pause menu → Settings → Display → HDR Calibration (Push Square).
  • Start with these base values if your TV supports ≥1,000 nits peak brightness:
    • Max Brightness: 2000
    • Black Levels: Start at 0 and increase slowly until details emerge (~20)
    • Paper White: 35
  • These settings should be adjusted visually rather than relying solely on numbers (HDTVTest).

Avoid Common Pitfalls

Be aware of these common errors:

  • GTA 6 doesn’t utilize console HDR calibrations - adjustments need to be made in-game (Push Square).
  • Test images may not perfectly reflect gameplay visuals, leading to miscalibration if relied upon too heavily (HDTVTest).
  • Ensure HGiG is on to avoid double tone mapping, which can result in washed-out visuals (Leonida Waypoint).

By meticulously following this setup and calibration sequence, you’ll achieve more vibrant and accurate HDR visuals in GTA 6, enhancing your overall gaming experience.

FAQ

What should I adjust first for better HDR in GTA 6?

Start by configuring your TV settings by enabling Game Mode and activating HGiG, followed by console and in-game calibrations.
