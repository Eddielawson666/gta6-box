Robbing a Store with a Crate of Beer? Here’s What’s Confirmed

## Short answer You *can* rob stores in GTA VI, either loud or stealth—sometimes with unexpected props like a crate of beer. That specific move isn’t officially confirmed, though. ## What’s actually confirmed Rockstar’s trailers and gameplay shows Jason and Lucia robbing stores—with masks, guns, quick grabs—sometimes solo, sometimes together. That part’s legit. Stores include shops, gas stations, jewelry outlets, payday loan spots. Stealth and loud approaches both exist. But nowhere do they show you strolling in with a crate of beer and slugging a clerk like a pub brawl. That one’s from players, not Rockstar. ## Watching live gameplay and interviews Rob Nelson—Rockstar’s gameplay guy—showed a stealth-leaning store robbery in an interview: sneaking in through the back, using a snap-gun to pick locks. No dramatic bottle throws there, just legit tools. Also, previews say stores aren’t just missions on a map—you walk into them and choose your own approach. ## What players are doing—and why to be skeptical There’s viral clips of folks chucking a crate of beer at clerks to knock ’em out. Funny to watch. But that’s player creativity in action. Rockstar didn’t show that. Until they confirm it in official footage or interviews, treat it as a cool glitch or emergent chaos, not a designed stealth tactic.
