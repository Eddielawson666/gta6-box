Where to Find Clothes in GTA VI – Store Map Guide

Short answer: There’s a big spread of shops across Vice City and Leonida, and yes you’ll need to explore. Sadly some of the details are still murky, but this is what we actually know.

Store count & types

Rockstar’s map filters show around 67 distinct clothing stores in Leonida, broken down by type: casual/street, sportswear, formal, souvenir/swimwear and thrift. Each has a little T‑shirt icon on your in‑game map when the fog lifts. There’s also an extra streetwear place called Stock 305, but that's only unlocked in the Ultimate Edition. These numbers aren’t just fan guesses – they come from actual in‑game map data. Another source reports ~70 pins mapped so far, with more expected as players push forward. It’s all stacking up pretty solidly.

For casual/streetwear you’ve got chains like Slinger, This / That, Mid, Suburban, Urban Legendz. Sportswear stores include Alpha Sports, Endzone and Eris. Need a suit? Head to Nola or Ferruccio Tucci. Swimsuits and tees? Look for Waves, Gifts A GoGo, Beach Please, Larry’s Lagoon and other souvenir/sun shops. Thrift options include Bargain World, Hamlet Thrift, Aline’s, Dr Froogal, Nifty Threads Island Wear.

Store behaviour & mechanics

Rockstar made it simple: all branches of the same brand carry the same inventory. So once you hit one Slinger or one Ferruccio Tucci, you've basically seen their stock. Stores usually have changing rooms, so you can swap outfits right there. Also cool: any outfit you buy gets sent to your safehouse wardrobe — and you can also stash outfits in your car’s trunk for quick changes on the go.

What’s still unknown

Exact addresses, store hours, and individual item prices aren’t officially confirmed by Rockstar. Some player‑compiled guides have price ranges (like Bargain World being cheap, Ferruccio Tucci expensive), but we haven’t seen those in developer statements or trailers — so treat them as rough estimates, not gospel.

Also, if you’re after rare-ish stuff like micro bikinis, those seem to be in specific swimwear shops like De’Luge in Ocean Beach, but Rockstar hasn’t outright said which stores have what. Community reports point there, but there's no official confirmation yet.

Quick nav tips

  • Open your in‑game map, use the “Style” filter and pick the type you want (casual, formal, sportswear, etc.) to see the T‑shirt icons.
  • Don’t go brand‑hopping unless you’re chasing location — same stock in each branch.
  • If you’ve got the Ultimate Edition, don’t forget about Stock 305 streetwear store in Stockyard — exclusive drips there.
  • Got a kick‑ass outfit? Save it — you can stash up to 15 favourite outfits for fast swaps.

Need to switch after a heist? Just hit the car trunk and slide into something less detectable.

FAQ

How many clothing stores are in GTA VI?

About 67 stores across five categories (casual/street, sportswear, formal, souvenir/swimwear, thrift), plus one Ultimate‑Edition‑only streetwear place (Stock 305).

Do all shops of the same brand have different clothes?

Nah. Every branch of a given brand stocks the same inventory, so visiting one is enough.

Can I change outfits on the go?

Yes. Stores usually let you change there, your wardrobe holds your buys, and your car trunk lets you switch on the fly.

Where do I get fancy or formal wear?

Hit Nola or Ferruccio Tucci stores. Those are the formal chains.
