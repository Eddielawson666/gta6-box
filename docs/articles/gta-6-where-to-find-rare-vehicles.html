Where to Find Rare Vehicles in GTA 6

Finding rare vehicles in GTA 6 involves exploring specific locations known for their unique and high-end car spawns. If you're in search of military vehicles like the Hauners Rhino tank or the Lazer fighter jet, visit the Mile Zero Air Station on Goose Key after completing Chapter 1's “Gone Fishing” mission. Additionally, Chapter 1 introduces the Imponte Phoenix and various aircraft in the Hamlet police impound lot (gta.guide).

Once you progress to Chapter 3, you can access Randy’s Wish List of supercars, which includes models like the Granforma Zorrusso and the Grotti Itali RSX. These vehicles require a Key Cloner to unlock and can be found across affluent areas such as Vice City, Capri, and Ambrosia (gta.guide).

For fans of custom finishes, the game includes numerous standout vehicles. A guide has cataloged over 280 unique vehicles, with about 225 marked on the in-game map across areas like Port Gellhorn and Ambrosia (gameta.gg). Community maps also highlight rare finds, like the elusive Truffade Thrax and Progen Royal Emerus, often marked with details about locks or alarms (gta6gps.com).

Keep an eye out for unexpected discoveries, such as the Annis Pixel Elegy hidden in a shipping container at the Capri Construction Yard, although its appearance seems inconsistent and unverified (reddit.com).

To collect these rare vehicles efficiently, check the in-game map for pinned spawns, progress the story to unlock more zones, and use tools like the Key Cloner. Exploring affluent neighborhoods and keeping up with community discoveries will also enhance your garage with unique vehicles.

FAQ

Where can I find military vehicles in GTA 6?

Military vehicles like the Hauners Rhino tank and the Lazer fighter jet are located at the Mile Zero Air Station on Goose Key, accessible after Chapter 1's "Gone Fishing" mission.

How do I access supercars in GTA 6?

From Chapter 3 onward, access high-end supercars from Randy's Wish List using a Key Cloner. These vehicles are found in upscale areas like Vice City and Capri.

Are there community resources for finding unique vehicles?

Yes, community maps track rare vehicles, providing details about location, locks, and alarm status.
