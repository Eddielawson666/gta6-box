How Long Will GTA VI Take to Beat?

Alright, mate, so you're wondering how long you're gonna be glued to the screen for GTA VI. Well, Rockstar hasn't given us a specific hour count, but based on their track record, you're probably looking at a hefty chunk of your life. Remember GTA V? That beast took around 30 to 40 hours just for the main storyline. Given the scope and hype around GTA VI, I wouldn't be surprised if it matches or even exceeds that.

During the demo I checked out, the map felt massive, and the story seemed thicker than a Sunday roast. So, expect a meaty journey.

What to Do After Beating the Main Story

Once you've smashed through the storyline, don't worry, the fun's not over. Rockstar's hinted at some juicy post-game content. Ever wondered why their games always have a cult following long after the credits roll? It's because they load 'em up with extras.

  • Online Multiplayer: If you've played GTA V, you know Rockstar knows how to nail online gameplay. They've already confirmed multiplayer for GTA VI. If you want the lowdown on what to expect, have a look at our multiplayer breakdown.
  • Side Missions & Activities: The official gameplay trailers showed off a bunch of side quests and mini-games. Think street racing, heists, and more.
  • Exploration: The world in GTA VI looks ripe for exploration. With new biomes and locations, just cruising around can be an adventure.

Still Hungry for More?

If you're the type who loves collecting every hidden gem, Rockstar's got you covered. Expect hidden outfits, cars, and bikes much like we've seen in previous installments. Curious about what treasures might be lurking in the game? Check out our comprehensive list of secrets.
