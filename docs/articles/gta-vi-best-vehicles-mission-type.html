GTA VI's Vehicle Arsenal: A Quick Look

Right off the bat, let's cut to the chase: Rockstar has teased a broad array of vehicles in the trailers and gameplay clips for GTA VI. From high-speed supercars to rugged off-roaders, there's something for every mission. However, specifics on which vehicle suits each mission type aren't fully out there yet. But don't worry; I've fished out some gems based on what's confirmed so far.

High-Speed Pursuits

If Rockstar's trailers are anything to go by, the Pegassi Osiris is making a grand comeback. Already seen zipping through sunlit boulevards, this beast is perfect for high-speed chases. It handles like a dream and looks even better. As of now, no other vehicle has been confirmed to match its speed and agility in a chase.

Off-Road Adventures

For those wild countryside missions, a vehicle like the BF Bifta seems to be your go-to. It's shown tearing through rugged terrain in a gameplay clip, and let's be honest, Rockstar knows how to design an off-roader that gets the job done. It's a must for the dirt tracks.

The Heists

Ah, the classic heists. Trailers have hinted at armored vehicles, but no specific models have been confirmed. Expect something along the lines of GTA V's Kuruma, but for now, it's speculation. A safe bet would be to look for something fast and armored once we know more.

Everyday Cruising

When you're just vibing around town, you're going to want something like the Declasse Voodoo. Spotted in the official gameplay footage, this ride brings back vintage vibes with a modern twist. It's not the fastest or the toughest but it has style in spades for those laid-back missions.

For long sessions I stick with the DualSense, grab one here.

Unconfirmed but Expected

Then there's the mystery category. Rockstar hasn't confirmed everything yet, but if GTA V is any indicator, expect a bunch of other vehicles tailored for specific roles. As the release date inches closer, keep an eye out for updates.

In GTA VI, picking the right vehicle for the job will likely be as crucial as ever. While the game isn't fully out yet, these glimpses into the vehicle lineup give us a solid starting point for planning our virtual escapades.
