GTA VI all ‘hidden’ weapons — what’s actually confirmed

Quick answer

There’s no full list of secret or hidden weapons in GTA VI yet. But Rockstar has officially confirmed exactly three by name, and a few more we’ve seen in trailers or gameplay.

What Rockstar named

These are in Rockstar’s own text — no guessing:

  • Hawk & Little Morgan Revolvers — a matching set for Jason and Lucía, shown in the Ultimate Edition copy.
  • Girardi ES9 — Jason’s pistol, name appears in Rockstar copy.
  • Klose K17 — Lucía’s pistol, same deal. That’s it so far.

Rockstar hasn’t released a full “hidden weapon” list. If it’s not in their text or shown in footage, we’re still guessing.

Weapons you’ve seen in action (but not always named)

From the Extended Look and trailers, there are a handful of actual weapons you’ve likely seen:

WeaponHow it appears

These match what Rockstar showed — “real weapons in use”, though most carry nicknames from the community, not official names.

So where are the truly hidden ones?

As of launch, Rockstar hasn’t whispered about secret stashes, unlock triggers, or hidden RPGs yet. People talk about RPGs or miniguns, but Rockstar hasn’t named them or shown them in any trailer or Extended Look.

So if you’re searching for hidden rocket launchers in a back alley or miniguns in a garage — sorry, no confirmation yet. Could be later DLC or timed unlocks, but we just don’t know.

The bottom line

You’ve got three pistols officially named. A handful of actual weapons you’ve seen in use. Everything else? Rockstar hasn’t confirmed it yet.
