How to Ride the Jet Ski with Cool Dog in GTA VI

Short answer: you can’t "join jet‑ski play" in a multiplayer mode—GTA VI is single‑player only at launch. But there is a quirky solo encounter where a pup on a jet ski rescues you, and you can hop aboard. Here’s the low‑down.

1. It’s a single‑player cheeky rescue, not a co‑op stunt

Rockstar’s confirmed: GTA VI launches as a single‑player experience with absolutely no multiplayer modes out of the box. If you’re thinking ‘play with Cool Dog’ in co‑op—that doesn’t exist yet. That’ll wait until they release the online version, likely in 2027 or later.

2. How the Cool Dog encounter actually works

Want to be saved by the coolest canine in gaming? Swim out into open water—away from your boat, away from the coast. No missions needed, no unlocks. Just swim, wait till you hear barking and engine noise, then use Focus mode to locate him. Hold L2 (on PlayStation) to interact, then hit Triangle/Y to climb on his jet ski. You’re riding with Cool Dog, mate.

3. What happens on the ride

If you stay on the jet ski, Cool Dog will take you somewhere. Sometimes it’s just back to shore. Other times he ferries you all the way to a little dog‑filled hideaway on Dog Island. There’s toys to play fetch with, and you’ll even find a wallet with some cash in it. Sunglasses might join your wardrobe. It’s a random, delightful side‑thing—no mission requirement.

4. What’s confirmed and what’s guesswork

  • Confirmed: GTA VI opens with just single‑player, no multiplayer yet. Jet skis and the Cool Dog rescue are in the game. The ride to Dog Island, the toys, the wallet rewards—all legit.
  • Not confirmed: Any formal co‑op play, competitive jet‑ski races, or future paid DLC featuring Cool Dog with your mates. Rockstar hasn’t said anything about that.

5. Gear tip

If you wanna stay comfortable while exploring all the water stuff—or going for long jet ski rides—get a solid controller. I’ve stuck with my DualSense and it’s a dream. You can grab one here.

Cheat sheet: Single‑player only for now. Swim far enough, wait, interact, enjoy a surreal dog rescue ride.
