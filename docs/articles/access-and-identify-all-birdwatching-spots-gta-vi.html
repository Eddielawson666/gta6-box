How to Access Birdwatching Spots & Identify All Birds in GTA VI

Short answer: you get a pair of binoculars early, use them to spot birds across Leonida, and the game logs each one in a wildlife journal. There are 60–62 species confirmed so far. The rest is up to your keen eye.

Unlocking Birdwatching

You get binoculars way early, in the Chapter 1 mission “Gone Fishing.” After that you can pull them anytime from your loadout—hold L1/LB and pick them. That’s it. That’s your entry into birdwatching.

How to Spot & Log Birds

  • Equip binoculars, zoom in with D‑Pad Up and hold R1/RB on the bird until it’s recorded.
  • Entries go into Progress > Animals under the Birds tab.
  • No killing, no capturing—just look and the game logs it.

How Many Birds Are There?

Rockstar’s pre-launch number is 62 bird species, but the in‑game Progress screen currently shows 60. Could be grouping or separate tracking under hunting. Nobody’s published the full list or exact spots yet.

Where to Look

Even without a guide, you can hunt your own spots:

  • Coasts, beaches, docks—great for gulls, pelicans, ibis.
  • Swamps, marshes, wetlands—herons, ducks, spoonbills, cranes.
  • Towns, parks, rooftops—crows, sparrows, parakeets.

Audubon-style field trials already show you can log around 50 birds in a few hours. Others found the final species—like an American Barn Owl—on a water tank at a rural town far north. All species are available from the start. Keep pushing into varied habitats.

Accuracy & Game Weirdness

Some in-game birds are spot‑on. You’ll see monk parakeets preening on wires, brown pelicans swooping by the water, immature laughing gulls that look and move right. But not everything’s realistic: golden eagles over nonexistent mountains, whooping cranes everywhere—even including odd calls swapped between species. GTA VI is not a birding sim—but the vibe is surprisingly close to Florida birding with a dash of chaos.

Pro Tips from a Fellow Gamer

  • Start birding from your safehouse deck—tons of easy picks right there.
  • Fanboats and swamp runs = high-value bird territory.
  • No story gating here—go anywhere, anytime.

FAQ

Do you need to finish the story to spot all the birds?

Nope. All bird species are available from the start—log as many as you like right after Chapter 1.

Is there a reward for logging all birds?

Rockstar hasn’t confirmed any bird-specific reward. Logging counts toward overall animal completion, but no bonus for birds alone.
