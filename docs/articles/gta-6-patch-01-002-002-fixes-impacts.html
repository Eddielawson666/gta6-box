GTA 6 Patch 01.002.002: Key Fixes and Player Impacts

GTA 6 Patch 01.002.002, released on November 23, 2026, addresses several critical bugs, enhancing stability and gameplay across PS5 and Xbox Series X|S (VI Field Guide). This patch primarily focuses on resolving game crashes, restoring autosave functionality, and ensuring essential phone calls for mission progression work correctly (Mission Dex).

A significant issue fixed by this update includes players being able to lose Wanted Levels, which were previously stuck, thus improving overall gameplay fluidity. The patch also addresses problems with partners failing to hand over bags in missions, allowing players to complete sales to fences smoothly (GTA6 Fixes).

While the weather glitch that caused persistent climate conditions, such as unending sun, has been mostly resolved, some PS5 players still report occasional issues. For these cases, clearing the system cache through Safe Mode may temporarily alleviate the problem (VI Field Guide).

However, not all issues have been fixed. The PS5 error code CE‑108255‑1 and performance-related concerns, such as frame rate limits, remain unresolved. Additionally, notorious glitches like the infinite money exploit continue to exist (GTA HUB).
