Is the Weather in GTA VI a Game-Changer?

Short answer: definitely. Rockstar's officially shown us glimpses of a weather system that's more than just eye candy. It's gameplay, mate.

Dynamic Weather Effects: Seen in Action

Rockstar's trailers have shown off a weather system that looks like it's been on steroids. We're talking about storms that seem to have a mind of their own. In one clip, you see lightning storms over the new Leonida region. This ain't just for show - expect it to mess with visibility and even affect car handling.

Realistic Environmental Changes

Okay, so here's where it gets interesting. Rockstar has confirmed that weather changes will influence the environment. Think mud getting slippery after rain. They teased this in a developer chat, hinting at roads becoming hazardous when wet. It's a bit like driving in GTA V, but cranked up a notch.
