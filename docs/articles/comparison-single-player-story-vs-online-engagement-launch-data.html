Quick verdict

Launch week data shows GTA VI’s single‑player story absolutely crushed it—record sales, millions immersed in Jason & Lucia’s tale. Online multiplayer isn’t even out yet. Early attention is all about the campaign.

Sales & Hours: Single‑player was king

Rockstar smashed records: 32.5 million copies sold before launch day, and a staggering $3.79 billion in day‑one retail sales. Biggest entertainment launch ever. It’s bonkers.

PlayStation alone saw over 39 million unique players in the first four days, with people averaging 17.4 hours in-game. On launch day, Circana data shows PS5 players sunk 513 minutes (8 hrs 33 mins) and Xbox folks about 494 minutes. Over half of tracked active console users played the game that day.

What about online?

Here’s the kicker—there’s no online mode at launch. Rockstar and Take‑Two haven’t said when it’s dropping. Analysts expect it in 2027, but that’s speculation, not fact.

So right now, all attention is on single‑player. Online hype is all anticipation, not engagement yet.

How does it stack up?

  • Single-player: Confirmed massive sales, huge playtime, record-breaking engagement.
  • Online: No mode at launch, zero launch‑week engagement. Future stuff.
