How to Find & Watch the ‘Extended Look’ on Netflix for GTA VI

TL;DR

You can watch GTA VI’s “An Extended Look” on Netflix if you’ve got a subscription. It premiered there August 27, 2026 at 3 p.m. ET, and six hours later it landed on YouTube and Rockstar’s site for free.

What’s the deal with this Netflix trailer?

Rockstar partnered with Netflix on this—An Extended Look is basically a half-hour slice of real PS5 in-game footage. It dropped first on Netflix globally, with subtitles in tons of languages. Six hours later, you could catch it on Rockstar’s official YouTube channel or the GTA VI site.

Runtime? Around 26–27 minutes. Not some short cinematic teaser, but proper gameplay—character swapping, action, world tour, the works.

How to actually find it on Netflix

  • Fire up Netflix on your device (TV, phone, browser, whatever).
  • Search for Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look.
  • It’s listed as its own 2026 title, about 26–27 minutes long.

Need it to stand out mid-stream? I grab a solid headset or controller—like this [one on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=gaming+headset&tag=gta6index-21)—so the immersion doesn’t drop.

What if you don’t have Netflix?

You can still watch it. Rockstar put the same video on their YouTube channel and the GTA VI site the same evening—six hours after Netflix. So yeah, Netflix got the early exclusive for a bit, but it’s not strictly required.

What we don’t know

There’s no official confirmation that this counts as Trailer 3 or reveals main story beats—it’s clearly gameplay-heavy, but Rockstar hasn't labelled it “Trailer 3.” If you're hoping for new cinematic storytelling or mission explanations, that hasn’t been confirmed.

So yeah—you want the full experience now, Netflix is the easiest. But you’re not locked out if you skip it. Rockstar made sure of that.

Oh, and one more thing—this is solid first-party content. Not fan edits or leaks. That alone makes it worth hunting down.
