How to Access & Decode Bad Bunny’s Cocoteo FM Lines

The quick answer

You can hear Bad Bunny’s lines—he’s El Martillo on Cocoteo FM—but there’s no official transcript yet. You’ll catch his voice between tracks in the game, and there’s a pro who records the station imaging, but you’re on your own decoding them for now.

Where you can actually hear him

Play GTA VI. Tune into Cocoteo FM in any ride or on foot with the earbuds or headphones Jason and Lucia can use. That’s how you’ll hit the in-game DJ banter—Bad Bunny as El Martillo, RaiNao as La Gata.

Rockstar confirms him in the credits—Bad Bunny is El Martillo, RaiNao is La Gata, and Issa Lopez is the imaging voice delivering shouts like the station name and reaction lines. But they haven’t put out any written lines or scripts, just the game audio. So if you want what they’re saying, you gotta listen yourself.

Decoding his lines

It’s just you and your ear. Spanish lines live in-game, but there’s no labelling or subtitles for radio chatter. Some fans are posting captures online—that’s your best bet for a quick decode. But Rockstar hasn’t released any script or even confirmed all of his lines, so everything beyond what you hear is speculation.

What’s confirmed vs. still unknown

  • Confirmed: Bad Bunny voices El Martillo, RaiNao is La Gata, Cocoteo FM is modern Latin station in GTA VI.
  • Confirmed: Issa Lopez is the studio imaging voice who says the station IDs with flow and attitude.
  • Unknown: Precise lines, their full text, translations, or any official transcription.
  • Unknown: Whether Bad Bunny wrote or improvised them, or if there’s any published breakdown—they haven't provided any.

Mid‑game tip: if you set up a clip recorder on your phone or PC and record while tuning in, you’ll snag those bits intact.
