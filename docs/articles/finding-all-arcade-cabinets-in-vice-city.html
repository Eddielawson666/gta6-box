How to Find All Arcade Game Cabinets in Vice City

Google AdSense

Short answer: yes, there’s confirmed arcade cabinets in Vice City—but only for one game so far. The other games are on the in-game home console. Let’s break it down.

What’s Actually Confirmed

Rockstar officially shows three playable games on the home JoySystem console: Tenebrae Lux, Street Trash III, and Time Jackers: Rewind. Those are accessed using the controller next to the TV in your safehouse. That’s locked during Chapter 2, then comes back Chapter 3 onward.
Only one arcade cabinet game is confirmed so far: Bang For Your Buck. You walk up to a physical cabinet in the city to play it. One spot we’ve seen for sure is outside Bargain World in Port Gellhorn. Rockstar hasn’t confirmed every single cabinet location yet.

Where to Look Around Vice City

Fans have mapped 31 cabinet machines across Leonida, and about 11 of those are in Vice City proper. Most of them show either Bang For Your Buck or Street Trash 3: Dezombifier (though Street Trash III is also on the home console). Cabinet locations live in places like bars, diners, convenience stores—especially Little Cuba, Port Gellhorn, Bohemia, Hamlet, Washington Beach, Watson Bay, and Downtown. So, bars first. If you’re in Vice City, check the local watering holes for that classic arcade glow.

How to 100% Them (If That’s Your Thing)

To hit the 100 % checklist, just start all four games—doesn’t matter if you quit right away. Want the trophy “Stick Fiddler”? Finish all levels of any one game. So you can either grind the whole of Bang For Your Buck on the cabinets or focus on one of the home games. Your call, bud.

What Isn’t Confirmed (Yet)

  • Exact spot-by‑spot list of every cabinet. Map sites have pins, but Rockstar hasn’t made an official list.
  • Any other arcade titles besides Bang For Your Buck in the wild.
  • Visual markers like neon signs or prompts beyond the cabinet itself.

My Two Cents

Honestly, Rockstar played it smart. Keeping the big three on your home console means less running around. But that cabinet hunt vibe? Classic GTA. Rolling into a neon‑lit bar and spotting a shooter game feels like Vice City IRL.

So, when you next cruise Vice City—hit up busy bars in Port Gellhorn, Little Cuba, Bohemia. Watch for cabinets and just walk up to ’em.

FAQ

Can you play all four arcade-style games on cabinets in Vice City?

No. Three games—Tenebrae Lux, Street Trash III, Time Jackers: Rewind—are only on the in‑home JoySystem console. Only Bang For Your Buck is confirmed on cabinets scattered around the city.

Where’s a known cabinet location for Bang For Your Buck?

One confirmed spot is outside Bargain World in Port Gellhorn—other cabinets exist, but Rockstar hasn’t officially mapped them yet.
