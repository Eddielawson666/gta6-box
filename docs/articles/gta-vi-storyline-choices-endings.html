How GTA VI Choices Shape Your Ending

Ever wondered how your decisions in GTA VI will affect the ending? You're not alone. Rockstar's keeping tight-lipped, but we've got some crumbs to chew on.

What Rockstar Has Said

In a recent interview, Rockstar confirmed that player choices in GTA VI will influence the storyline and potentially the ending. However, they’ve been mum on the specifics. So, yes, expect some twists and turns based on what you decide. But how deep this influence goes? That's still a bit of a mystery.

Past Patterns Give Clues

Think back to GTA V. Remember those final missions where you got to decide the fate of your character? It's likely Rockstar will crank that up a notch in GTA VI. Multiple endings are pretty much a given, but how many and how they differ is the million-dollar question.

Trailer Hints

Seen the trailers? They show a lot of action and drama. But if you squint, you might catch some hints of moral choices. Rockstar loves adding layers, and the trailers suggest more than just shootouts. Choices about alliances, betrayals, and those grey areas we love in GTA.

Gameplay and Choices

Based on the gameplay shown, choices aren't just in dialogue. It's in the heists you choose, the people you help or screw over, and the chaos you create. It's dynamic, and it's looking like every decision will count towards how the story wraps up.

What We Don’t Know Yet

Details are still under wraps. Will there be a single major choice or multiple smaller ones adding up? Rockstar hasn't confirmed this yet. We might have to wait until release day to see how deep the rabbit hole goes.
