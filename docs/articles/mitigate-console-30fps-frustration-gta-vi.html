Taming the 30 fps Beast in GTA VI

Short answer

Yeah, GTA VI is targeting about 30 fps on all consoles at launch. Blank. No performance mode, no officially confirmed 60 fps or 40 fps option yet.

But you can still ease the grind. Here's what you can actually tweak.

What’s confirmed (and what isn’t)

Rockstar says GTA VI is built to push current‑gen hardware to the absolute limit, aiming for 30 fps on PS5 (including Pro) and Xbox Series X|S. No performance vs quality modes announced, no 60 fps mode confirmed yet. It’s a target, not a lock—so drops can still happen in busy scenes.

That 60 fps on Pro rumor? Still unconfirmed. And the idea of a 40 fps middle ground? Pure speculation—for now.

What you actually can do

  • TV settings matter more now than ever. Turning on VRR (Variable Refresh Rate)? Game‑changer. It smooths things without altering the game. If your console resets after loading, try cold‑booting another app or game to reduce hitching.
  • HDR calibration—don’t just slap default. Getting your calibration right makes motion feel sharper, even if the frame rate doesn't budge.
  • Consider leaning into immersion. GTA VI’s movement is weighty, animations deliberate. If it’s well‑done—and reviewers say it mostly is—the slow feel kinda helps disguise the framerate.

Why no 60 fps yet?

Rockstar’s tech guys said the world they built is too complex—tons of systems interacting in real time—and you only have ~33 ms per frame. They chose 30 fps to juggle presentation, physics, AI, assets and all that without breaking stuff.

No performance mode is confirmed at launch, though history suggests one could drop later—GTA V got 60 fps on PS5 down the line. But don’t bank on it yet.

Final take

It sucks, I know. PC gamers have it easier. But if you lean into what’s confirmed—VRR, HDR tweaks, staying immersed—you can make 30 fps feel less punitive. Check your settings. Give your TV a chance to help. Own the grind, mate.

FAQ

Will GTA VI ever get 60 fps on console?

Not confirmed. Rockstar hasn’t announced any performance mode or higher frame‑rate option at launch. It might happen later, based on history, but right now it’s only speculation.

Does PS5 Pro do better than the base PS5 in terms of fps?

No higher fps confirmed. PS5 Pro does get better clarity through PSSR and ray‑traced shadows, but frame‑rate stays the same—30 fps target only.

Can switching cold‑boot trick the game into running faster than 30 fps?

There’s chatter about glitches where cold‑booting another app might make it feel faster, but that’s not confirmed or reliable. Use VRR instead—it’s legit.
