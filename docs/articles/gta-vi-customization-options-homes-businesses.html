GTA VI Customization: Homes and Businesses

So, you're wondering about customizing homes and businesses in GTA VI? Here's the lowdown: Rockstar's kept details close to the chest, but we know a few things. Let's dive into what's confirmed and what's still shrouded in mystery.

What We Know: Official Announcements

According to Rockstar's announcements and some juicy tidbits from official trailers, GTA VI is promising an expansion on property customizations. Homes aren't just for crashing after a heist anymore. The trailers hint at sharper interiors and potential upgrades. But, specifics? Still waiting on those.

Business Ventures: Time to Get Entrepreneurial?

Rockstar teased business ownership as a big feature. We're talking owning and running your own establishments. The extent of customization—like managing aesthetics or operations—isn't fully detailed. But if Rockstar's past style tells us anything, expect some fascinating depth here.

The Mystery of Interior Customization

Ever wondered if you'll be able to deck out your crib with the same finesse you do your rides? As of now, Rockstar hasn't confirmed detailed interior customization. The trailers suggest some freedom, but how much is still up in the air.

What About Exteriors?

While interior flair is a mystery, exterior customization might offer more. We've seen glimpses of modifiable business fronts, but we're in the dark about specifics. Be prepared for some surprises here.
