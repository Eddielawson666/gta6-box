Short answer up front

GTA VI launched November 19, 2026 as a single‑player game. Rockstar has said nothing official about an online mode yet. All talk of GTA VI Online dropping in 2027 is speculation—or Twitch CEO chatter—not a Rockstar announcement.

Rockstar’s actual stance

Both PlayStation and Xbox stores describe GTA VI as a “single‑player experience.” Rockstar and Take‑Two never mentioned multiplayer at launch. No name. No date. Totally radio silent on online so far.

The existing GTA Online (that’s for GTA V) continues. Take‑Two has said they’ll keep supporting it going forward—not shutting it down just because VI exists.

What the press and execs are saying (but it’s not official)

Twitch CEO Dan Clancy casually said after talking with Rockstar that he expects a “bigger bump” when multiplayer arrives—meaning he expects GTA VI Online in 2027. But again—he’s not Rockstar, and that’s not a confirmed release window.

Blips in the PlayStation database like “launchMultiplayerActivity” popped up. Could hint at work being done behind the scenes. But none of it adds up to a confirmation.

Your GTA legend’s take
