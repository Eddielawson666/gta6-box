Here's the Quick Scoop

Rockstar has been tight-lipped about GTA VI's hidden outfits, cars, and bikes. But from what we've seen in trailers and interviews, there are definitely a few to look out for. Let's dive into what we know and what we're guessing based on past GTA games.

Hidden Outfits

So far, Rockstar hasn't revealed much about hidden outfits. We saw a few in the trailers, like the classic heist suits and some snazzy streetwear, but nothing that's explicitly 'hidden'. If GTA V is anything to go by, you'll probably unlock these by completing missions or achieving certain milestones. But for now, all we can do is wait and see.

Secret Cars Worth Finding

Vehicles are a huge part of the GTA experience, and GTA VI is no different. From what Rockstar's shown, there are some sweet rides to look forward to. The trailer gives us a glimpse of sleek sports cars and unique classic models that scream 'hidden gem'. But the real question is, are there secret cars stashed away like in previous games? Rockstar hasn't confirmed any, but given their track record, you can bet there will be.

Bikes: Two-Wheeled Wonders

If you're more of a two-wheel fanatic, you're in luck. The footage so far shows a range of motorcycles from dirt bikes to cruisers. While not specifically labeled as 'hidden', these bikes could be part of your garage if you know where to look. Again, nothing is confirmed yet, but fingers crossed for some hidden treasures.

What's Still Up in the Air

While we've got some hints from trailers and interviews, a lot about GTA VI's hidden items is still a mystery.
