Quick answer

You’ve got four legit mini‑games tucked inside GTA VI—three on the JoySystem console at Jason’s place, plus a fourth arcade cabinet you can hunt down. Some are proper meat‑and‑potatoes games with puzzles, shooting, roguelike bits... not just quick throwaways.

The setup

JoySystem (or JoyStation, depending who you ask) is the in‑game console that pops up in Chapter 1 during the “Feng Shui” mission. It looks like a cheeky PS5 knock‑off next to the TV in Jason’s home. You can sit down (Triangle on Play or Y on Xbox) to launch the games. Rock it in Chapter 1, it's locked in Chapter 2, then opens again from Chapter 3 onward. Perfect for when you need a break from the main heist stuff.

The four mini‑games

  • Tenebrae Lux: black‑and‑white, first‑person walking‑sim puzzle—pretty arty, kinda creepy, lighthouse vibes and number puzzles.
  • Street Trash 3: Dezombifier: top‑down zombie twin‑stick shooter with XP and roguelike card upgrades—think Dead Nation but tighter and with unlocks.
  • Time Jackers: Rewind: cyber‑retro top‑down shooter where you bend time, chase shards and race the clock. Tactical but still actiony.
  • Bang For Your Buck: only playable on arcade cabinets dotted around Leonida—classic light‑gun gallery style, hunt deer but dodge the doe. One confirmed spot is outside Bargain World in Port Gellhorn.

What makes them worth it

These aren’t throwaway distractions. They have custom menus, cutscenes, gameplay loops. You can sink real hours—some reviewers did with the zombie one alone. There’s a trophy called Stick Fiddler if you actually finish one of them, but for 100% you just need to start all four.

Rockstar built them with an internal team. They’re full‑tilt mini‑games, not placeholders. Tenebrae Lux even pokes fun at artsy indie puzzle games—lovingly, not cynically.

Heads‑up

If you want the checklist done early, plan around Chapter 2 where the home console’s offline for a bit. The arcade cabinet version of Bang For Your Buck may not be at your safehouse—you’ll have to roam.

Still a bit fuzzy?

We know the games, how to open them, where to find that cabinet. But what about high‑score leaderboards, DLC, extra levels? Nothing confirmed yet. Rockstar hasn’t said anything on that. When they do—bet I’ll tell you.

FAQ

Do I have to finish every JoySystem mini‑game for 100%?

Nope. To hit that checklist item, you just need to start each one of the four games. But if you actually finish one game, you unlock the Stick Fiddler trophy.

When can I play these JoySystem games?

They unlock after the Chapter 1 "Feng Shui" mission. You lose access in Chapter 2, but it’s back from Chapter 3 onward.

Where can I play Bang For Your Buck?

That one’s on arcade cabinets around Leonida, not on the home console. A known machine sits outside Bargain World in Port Gellhorn.
