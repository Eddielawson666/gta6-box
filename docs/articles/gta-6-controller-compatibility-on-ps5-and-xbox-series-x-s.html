GTA 6 Controller Compatibility on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S

If you're gearing up to play GTA 6 on your PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X/S, you'll want to know which controllers offer the best experience. For PS5, the DualSense Wireless Controller is the best choice. It supports all of the game’s features, including haptic feedback and adaptive triggers. Rockstar and Sony have confirmed these features are fully integrated into GTA 6. The GTA VI Limited-Edition DualSense controllers offer the same functions with a stylish twist, released on November 19, 2026 (PlayDesk). Avoid using PS4 controllers as they are not compatible with PS5 games like GTA 6 (GTA6-Life).

On the Xbox Series X/S side, the standard Xbox Wireless Controller is fully supported. Alongside it, both the Xbox Elite Series 2 and the Adaptive Controller are compatible (TechYorker). However, these controllers don't offer specific GTA 6–centric integrations like the PS5. No GTA VI–themed controller has been released for Xbox, so the options here are mainly about gameplay preference rather than added features (PlayDesk).

Third-party controllers are mostly compatible if they're officially licensed for either console. For PS5, they won't provide the unique DualSense functionalities, while for Xbox, look for the "Designed for Xbox" label to ensure baseline compatibility (TechYorker).
