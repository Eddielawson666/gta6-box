Short answer

You unlock birdwatching early—right in Chapter 1, “Gone Fishing.” You get binoculars and those let you zoom in on birds, log them in a wildlife logbook. There are over 60 bird species confirmed so far, though the exact list and any rewards? Rockstar hasn’t shared those details yet.

1. How you get birdwatching going

From what’s actually been shown, the moment you kick off the story in Chapter 1 (“Gone Fishing”), you get binoculars—literally thrown at you during the mission. Right after, you can pull them out anytime via your weapon wheel, aim (hold RB/R1), zoom with D‑Pad Up, and log whatever bird is in view. It just clicks—the mechanics hit fast, no hunting for a special menu.

2. What’s in the logbook

Rockstar put in a wildlife logbook that tracks animals you study. Birdwatching flows into that. Reporters and early players count “over 60” or “62” bird species—Rockstar’s Rob Nelson mentioned 62, but the Progress screen in-game seems to show 60 bird types locked or listed. So it’s probably around 60–62, but they haven’t shared a full roster yet.

3. What counts for completion

The logbook entries count toward your animal completion stats. You need to study 100 out of 255 animals overall to hit 100 % game completion—and birds are in the mix. However, we’ve seen no sign there’s a specific reward for filling the bird section, like a special outfit or bonus. Doesn’t mean there isn’t one—but as of now, Rockstar hasn’t confirmed anything extra for zeroing out the birds.

4. What we still don’t know

  • No full list of bird species or where each lives—Rockstar hasn’t published one yet.
  • No idea if watching a rare bird unlocks anything special.
  • No secret zones or “hidden bird missions” revealed. Everything we’ve heard is from trailers, in‑game screens and dev comments—nothing beyond that.

For what it’s worth, this feature seems built for players who enjoy hanging back, zooming with binoculars, and ticking off species. It’s not central to the narrative—just a cool side‑hustle that amps up immersion in Leonida.
