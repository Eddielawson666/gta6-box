Short answer

If you want to nail those spontaneous world events and side missions in GTA VI, it’s mostly about exploring with your eyes peeled, saving smart, and treating each moment like a surprise. Rockstar didn’t publish a list or markers—it's on you to spot them.

What we actually know

Rockstar has confirmed there’s no map or checklist for these things. Random events just happen as you explore, unmarked. You’ll see weird stuff—like a lawyer in a dumpster taking pills—or get plopped into odd scenes, and you choose how to react. Rockstar said these encounters blur into the open‑world and mission flows. Some trigger only once. You’re on your own to find them. No marker, no map icon. They just happen when you’re around. Pause, look, respond. That’s the trick.

How to improve your odds

  • Drive around, don’t fast‑travel. Events pop up when you're moving through the world, especially at night. The map won’t tell you about them.
  • Watch for odd behaviour—crowds, vehicles stopped, shouts, subtitles. These are more reliable cues than chasing icons.
  • Save before engaging. Some events vanish after one play‑through. You may want to experiment with different choices.
  • Don’t just glance and bail. Brief engagement can extend cooldown so they barely ever reappear. Full interaction means they’re gone—but that’s how you unlock offbeat stories.
  • Time of day matters. Word is, a lot happens in the evening or night. The world evolves during visits days apart, and characters can come back with new lines.

The stuff we don’t know yet

We don’t have a total event count. Rockstar dropped hints of “over a thousand interactive encounters” and hundreds of ambient ones—but no official registry exists. We don’t know triggers, reward consistency, or repeatability. Until Rockstar says more, expect surprises—but don’t count on systematic drops or repeat runs.

A bit of opinion—because you asked

Honestly, they nailed the energy here. It’s not like past GTAs where you just mash menus. Now the world’s alive. But that also means you miss a ton if you rush missions. Take your time. It’s worth stopping to listen or investigate. That’s when magic happens.
