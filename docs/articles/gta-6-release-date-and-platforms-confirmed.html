GTA 6 Release Date and Platforms Confirmed

Gaming fans, rejoice! After years of anticipation, Grand Theft Auto VI has officially launched worldwide. Rockstar Games announced that the game became available on November 19, 2026, marking a major moment for fans of the series. Let’s dive into the confirmed details about its release and supported platforms.

Official Release Date

November 19, 2026, is the date when GTA 6 was released globally. This confirmation comes directly from Rockstar Games, supported by documentation from Take-Two Interactive, the parent company. Both sources listed this date as the official launch of the game, putting to rest years of speculation and excitement for what this new installment would bring (Rockstar Games)(Take-Two Interactive).

Confirmed Platforms

Grand Theft Auto VI is currently available on the following platforms:

  • PlayStation 5, including the PS5 Pro variant
  • Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S

Both PlayStation and Xbox's official channels list the game for these current-generation consoles, highlighting the technological advancements leveraged by the hardware capabilities of the PS5 and Xbox Series X|S (PlayStation)(Static Coast).

What About Other Platforms?

As for other platforms, a PC release has yet to be officially announced. There are rumors based on unnamed sources suggesting a PC version could appear in Q1 2027, but this information is not substantiated by Rockstar or Take-Two at this time (Vicedex). Discussions around potential releases for Xbox Game Pass, PlayStation Plus, older consoles like PS4, Xbox One, or other new systems like the rumored Switch 2 remain purely speculative.

The Characters: Jason and Lucia

With the release confirmed, players can now immerse themselves in the world of GTA 6 alongside the new protagonists, Jason and Lucia. Their adventures are set to bring fresh narratives and gameplay mechanics, capitalizing on the advanced technology of current-gen consoles.

For more updates on future expansions or potential platform announcements, keep an eye on Rockstar's official channels. You can also explore our guide on what to expect from GTA VI's online mode.

Enjoy the experience in Vice City through the eyes of Jason and Lucia as you uncover the new chapters in this iconic series. Stay tuned for more updates on this thrilling release!
