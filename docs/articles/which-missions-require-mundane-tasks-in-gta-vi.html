Which GTA VI Missions Actually Make You Do Chores?

Google AdSense

Short answer? A tiny handful. Rockstar didn’t turn GTA into The Sims—but yeah, there are a few missions where you’re literally buying groceries or unclogging a sink.

Confirmed Mundane Missions

  • "Pick up Groceries & Drop Off Groceries" – optional Chapter 1 errand from Lucia. You grab three items from a mini‑mart and carry them into Jason’s house to raise Lucia’s opinion of you. No cash reward. Totally confirmed by multiple sources.
  • “Pipework” – play as Jason, plunge and open a tenant’s kitchen sink to collect Mandy’s rent. You’re literally fixing a clogged sink to get rent. Confirmed as a domestic‑style mission.
  • “Feng Shui” – again in Chapter 1, you help move living‑room furniture with Cal for Lucia. Pure chore. Confirmed too.
  • “Just Moved In” – Lucia mission in Chapter 2. Buy eight items from a store for the motel room, costs money, but ups relationship score. Confirmed as mundane shopping task.
  • “Building a Life” – help Lucia assemble and then disassemble a coffee table, glass top and all. All chore. Confirmed.
  • “Play Nurse” (Take Care of Jason) – Lucia watches over Jason until he recovers at the motel. Not exciting, but it’s part of the story.

That’s about it. Out of ~91 story missions, only 3 are domestic chores, and 5 more when you include the story tasks between missions. That’s like 3% of the total, mostly early chapters. From Chapter 3 onward? Zero chores—just crime sprees, big moments, not vacuuming or groceries.

Why It Feels Like There’s More

Some clips got shared online of Jason renovating apartments, taking out trash, working out, even fixing a rotting roof. But most of that isn’t mission‑based. These are open‑world, slice‑of‑life bits you can do between the big missions. Not chores assigned by Lucia.

So What’s Confirmed, What’s Not?

  • Confirmed: the grocery run, sink fix, furniture shift, shopping, coffee‑table build, nursing Jason. All in Chaps 1–2, all basic daily life tasks with no guns.
  • Not confirmed: things like roofing, painting, taking out actual trash, full apartment reno—players have done them, but they’re not mission objectives. Rockstar never said those are missions.

Final Word

Yeah, it’s funny seeing GTA characters doing chores. But Rockstar’s only put chores in early missions, and mostly to build character, not fill the map. It’s a blip, not a lifestyle change for the series.

FAQ

Are there chores in later chapters?

So far, chores are only in Chapters 1–2. Nothing confirmed later on.

Do chores pay money in GTA VI?

Groceries cost what you spend, no payout. Others like furniture, sink fix, nursing—no cash reward, mostly just relationship or story progress.
