How to preload GTA VI without it crushing your storage

Short answer

You can start preloading GTA VI on November 10, 2026 (PS5 local midnight, Xbox 00:00 GMT). Rockstar says you’ll need up to 212 GB of free space to download it—but that’s just the preload file, not necessarily the full installed size.

When does preload start (and how)

Rockstar confirms: digital pre-load kicks off November 10. PS5 users at your local midnight, Xbox Series X|S globally at 00:00 GMT. Physical boxes? They land November 12 and include a download code you can use to preload too.

Set your console to auto-download (PS5: Rest Mode; Xbox: Sleep mode). Otherwise you’ll have to kick it off manually.

Storage—what does “up to 212 GB” mean?

That “up to 212 GB” is the official number from Rockstar’s support page. It’s the space you need available to begin the preload—not necessarily what the final installed game will be. That covers all platforms in one vague figure—they didn’t break it down by console.

On PS5, some trackers saw about 210 GB—but that wasn’t official and now Rockstar’s 212 GB is the safe bet.

Heads-up: early patches starting November 16 and 23 have already added around 600 MB on PS5 and 1.6 GB on Xbox. So leave a smidge of extra room for updates.

Tips to optimize your storage

  • PS5: Move games to USB or M.2 SSD if you’re low on space, but you can’t play them from there—you’ll need to shift them back to internal before running GTA VI.
  • Xbox: Removing games doesn’t delete your progress, so clear the way if you’re tight on room.
  • New storage? A fast SSD or expansion card helps—but don’t buy assuming download sizes beyond 212 GB—that’s your planning number.

What we don’t know yet

  • Exact install size per platform? Rockstar hasn’t confirmed any breakdown.
  • PC requirements? No one has announced if GTA VI’s even coming to PC yet.

Honestly, it’s a beast to prep for. But once the preload’s done you won’t scramble at launch night. Clear around 220 GB if you can, and you’ll cruise.
