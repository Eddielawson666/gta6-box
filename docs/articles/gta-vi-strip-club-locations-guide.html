GTA VI strip club locations – what’s legit so far

Google AdSense

Short answer: there are exactly three strip clubs in GTA VI so far, two in Vice City and one out in Port Gellhorn. Two are open early, one you’ve gotta wait for.

Which clubs are in the game?

Confirmed strip clubs:

  • NINE1NINE – in Downtown Vice City, near The Vault nightclub. Accessible pretty early.
  • Jack of Hearts – in Crosstown, Vice City. Locked until around Chapter 3.
  • Delights Cabaret – in Port Gellhorn, up north. Cheap and rough-and-ready, but a fair drive from the start.

You’ll also see other venues under the “Clubs” filter on your map – The Vault (nightclub) and The Rusty Anchor (tavern) – but they’re not strip clubs.

What can you actually do at them?

All three let you request private dances (yeah, GTA style). Prices vary: Delights is the bargain, NINE1NINE mid-tier, Jack of Hearts highest. Delights even has pool tables and stage dances too.

Rockstar hasn’t released an official price list. The numbers floating around (like $40, $300, $1,000 plus tips) are from players testing it out after launch; treat them as “looks about right,” not gospel.

When can you get in?

Delights and NINE1NINE are available starting in early chapters, anytime of day. Jack of Hearts waits until Chapter 3 when story progress opens the doors.

Why the weird locations?

NINE1NINE is a flashy high-end joint with VIP lounges. Delights is run-down and local. Jack of Hearts has a music studio upstairs and leans into that hip-hop / XXX vibe. Hell, even the map filter marks them blue under "Activities > Clubs"—nice touch.

What Rockstar hasn’t confirmed… yet

  • Exact dance costs and tip increments.
  • Whether private dances impact relationships or story beats.
  • Any extra services beyond stage dancing, pool, and private dances.

Word is they really went all out researching nightlife—they actually visited strip clubs in Florida during development. Adds to the feeling these places aren’t just window dressing.

So yeah, if you’re itching to blow cash on a private dance, Delights is where your wallet wants to hit first. Swanky tastes head to NINE1NINE once you’re flush. And Jack of Hearts? Worth the wait, assuming the story drops you in tight.
