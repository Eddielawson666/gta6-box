Short answer

You’ll hear Bad Bunny as the host “El Martillo” on the Latin station Cocoteo FM in GTA VI. He introduces songs like “EoO,” “Otra Noche en Miami,” and more. The full lines are in the live game and credits, but Rockstar hasn’t published them outside the game.

Where to catch Bad Bunny’s radio bits

He’s on Cocoteo FM—Rockstar’s modern‑Latin station (bachata, merengue, dembow, reggaeton). In the credits, he’s listed as El Martillo, and RaiNao is La Gata. That’s official. Voice‑match reports confirm Bad Bunny plays El Martillo and RaiNao plays La Gata.

You’ll catch him introducing tracks like “EoO” by Bad Bunny itself, “Otra Noche en Miami,” plus others in‑game as you drive or walk around Vice City. The credits name the tracks “Otra Noche en Miami,” “EoO,” and “Mojabi Ghost” with Tainy among his credits.

What we don’t know yet

Rockstar hasn’t released his full quotes or scripts. You’ve gotta hear him in‑game to get them. No official transcript or audio dump so far. As of now, that’s on you—crank the stereo, record your session.

Also, we don’t know how many times he talks, what he says beyond song intros, or if he ad‑libs. Only live gameplay reveals that.
