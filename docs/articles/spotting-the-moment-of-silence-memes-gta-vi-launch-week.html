Short answer

If you spotted people posting “moment of silence” during the GTA VI launch, yeah, that was them lamenting the end of the “We got X before GTA 6” meme. It skyrocketed in mentions at launch, and everyone just stopped using that joke—forever. Crazy how culture shifts overnight, right?

What’s a “moment of silence” meme?

People would jokingly say “moment of silence” to mark the death of the old meme. This old meme was about listing random, improbable things that happened before GTA VI finally dropped—both to mock the wait and to bond over how long this took. By release day, the joke got its own funeral.

Why everyone noticed it launch week

During launch week, mentions of “moment of silence” jumped from just over a dozen to nearly 150—around a 1000% increase. It was literally everywhere on social media as people acknowledged the meme’s passing. That trend was one of the fastest rising keyword clusters next to debates over 30 fps. Angry, celebratory, nostalgic, all at once.

Why did the meme die?

  • Once GTA VI was out, you couldn’t say “We got X before GTA 6” without it sounding outdated. That format thrived on the wait—it had no reason to live beyond launch.
  • Creators leaned into the mourning trope. Sketches and short videos pretended to solemnly pay respects to the meme’s run. That only confirmed it was officially retired.

The end of an era, and people treated it like one.

How to spot them

Context and atmosphere

Launch week was full of meme madness—"GTA flu" (calling in sick), “towel-wrapped PS5s,” dusty consoles, countdowns, “goodbye GTA V” themes. But this “moment of silence for old memes” thing had that weird mix of nostalgia and collective catharsis. Everyone knew that format had reached its shelf-life.

It’s oddly poetic: the internet mourning a joke until it’s got nothing left to say.
