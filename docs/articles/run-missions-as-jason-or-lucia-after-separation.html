The short answer

Once Chapter 2 rolls around—right after the mission “Coffee Break” (some say it’s “Breakfast Run,” but either way, it’s early in Chapter 2)—you unlock free switching between Jason and Lucia in open world. Hold right on the D‑pad plus R1 (PS5) or RB (Xbox) to swap. But yes, some missions still lock you into one character.

1. When it becomes available

You can’t toggle them right away. The game starts you off with Jason (Lucia appears only in story flashes). Switching opens up in Chapter 2 after the narrative lines say so—most sources point to right after “Coffee Break,” others mention “Breakfast Run,” but they agree it’s early Chapter 2.

2. How to switch

Once unlocked, in free roam just hold D‑pad right and hit R1 (PS5) or RB (Xbox). That brings up the partner menu—pick the other lead and it switches right there. You’ll see your partner’s portrait unless they’re busy or the mission has rules.

3. Why some missions block the switch

Some story missions are designed to force you to stay as one character—Jason or Lucia—so swapping is disabled. These missions are marked with a J or L icon on the map. In other cases, the game switches you automatically at key moments if that’s how the narrative goes.

4. What happens once you're separated

After Lucia separation—say she’s off doing her thing—you still can switch back when you're in free roam, as long as the story structure allows it. But watch out, there are reports (some players on Reddit) that after Chapter 3 side missions players got stuck as one character and couldn't switch back. That sounds like a bug, not intended design. If it happens, try saving, reloading, or stepping away and reloading—they got it unstuck that way.

5. A mate’s take (that’s me)

Honestly, Rockstar nailed the feel of dual leads. Switching feels seamless. But yeah, when that J or L icon’s showing, you’re in solo territory. And if you hit a glitch and can’t swap post-Lucia separation, don’t panic—save, restart, reload, and you’re usually back in the game.

Quick checklist

  • Finish Chapter 1, get to Chapter 2.
  • Do the mission “Coffee Break” (or “Breakfast Run”).
  • Hold D‑pad right + R1/RB in free roam to switch.
  • Ignore when map icon shows J or L—that’s a solo-only mission.
  • Hit weird bugs post-Lucia separation? Save, reload, walk away.

FAQ

Can I switch between Jason and Lucia anytime after Chapter 2?

In open world, yeah—as long as the mission isn’t locked to one character. But if you hit a mission marked J or L on the map, you’re stuck playing that character through it.

I’m stuck as one character after Lucia separated—any fix?

Sounds like a bug some players saw. Try saving, exiting, reloading. That’s what sorted it for them.
