Short answer

Yeah, GTA VI actually lets you birdwatch—and there’s a literal bird‑nest Easter egg in Vice City. Lots of confirmed wildlife, a few confirmed birding mechanics, but plenty Rockstar hasn’t spelled out yet.

Birdwatching? Straight up.

You start with binoculars during your intro. Pretty soon after that, you can start spotting birds, logging them in the Progress menu. Audubon says there’s at least 60 bird species to find—including spoonbills and pelicans. It even tracks them in-game. That’s real, that’s in the game, not a rumor.

Vice City wildlife—confirmed species

Trailer footage shows flamingos, pelicans, sea turtles, alligators, iguanas. Rockstar confirmed those species in official trailers and media. It’s more than window dressing—makes Leonida feel wild.

The Painted‑Egg Nest Easter Egg

Climb the tallest radio tower in Vice City. At the top, you’ll find a bird’s nest with painted eggs. Grab one, throw it off. You get a little cut‑scene gag.

Not just a rumor—reported by multiple outlets, players have actually done it in-game. It’s a proper Easter egg. No reward, just a small laugh.

What’s not confirmed (yet)

  • Other bird‑related Easter eggs? Rockstar hasn’t named any more. If someone shares a wild theory, say so—but note it’s not officially confirmed.
  • Exact list of all 60 bird species? We don’t have that yet. Audubon counted them but Rockstar hasn’t shared all the names.
  • Specific hunting/spawning locations per bird species? Rockstar hasn’t said.

Ideas for a birdwatching session

Start Chapter 1. You get your binoculars early—pull them out next time you’re out in the swamp or around docks. Scan birds, log ’em. After you’ve spent some time there, make your way to that radio tower—worth the climb.

Birdwatch in the daylight, then at night climb that tower and make some bird‑egg mischief. Nice mix.

FAQ

How many bird species are in GTA VI?

Audubon tracked at least 60 species in the Progress screen—though pre‑launch said 62, the game only shows 60 so far.

Is the painted‑egg nest Easter egg real?

Yes. Climb the tallest radio tower in Vice City, grab a painted egg from a nest, throw it off, and a gag cut‑scene plays.
