GTA 6 PC Port: Project Leonida and the Take-Two DMCA Takedown

As of November 28, 2026, Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6) has no officially announced native PC edition or PC release date. The game launched on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Meanwhile, Project Leonida, an unofficial PC port effort associated with leaked console game files, has become the subject of a copyright takedown by Take-Two Interactive. See Rockstar's official platform information.

What happened to Project Leonida?

On November 24, Take-Two submitted a copyright complaint under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA). According to Decrypt's November 25 report, GitHub processed the request across a network of 3,007 repositories, including the original project and related copies. Take-Two alleged that the project infringed its copyright and breached Rockstar's terms of service.

The removal of repositories is not a court ruling on the merits of the copyright claims. Reports also describe disruption to the project's Discord community, but that does not establish that Take-Two directly requested each separate platform action.

Does the unofficial port mean GTA 6 is coming to PC sooner?

No official release schedule can be inferred from these developments. An unofficial technical demonstration is not the same as a supported, commercially released PC game. Claims circulating online do not establish that a complete, stable or safe PC version is available.

What PC gamers should know

Be cautious with websites claiming to offer an early PC edition. Unverified downloads can expose users to malware, credential theft or scams. Check Rockstar's official announcements instead. GTA6 Index reports on these developments for informational purposes and does not provide unauthorized game files, download locations or installation instructions.

Rockstar has not published PC system requirements in its current platform information. If you are considering a hardware upgrade, buy for your existing games and workloads rather than assuming compatibility with an unreleased PC edition.
