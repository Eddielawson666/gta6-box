Your criminal rep in GTA VI

Quick verdict: Rockstar tracks how brutal you are, and that stays with you long after the cops cool off. You’ve got five tiers: Professional, Hardened, Violent, Psycho, and Irredeemable. Once you're Irredeemable – that’s it. Game over on clean rep. So here’s how to stay sharp or climb back fast.

1. Stay tidy—incapacitate, don’t maim

Shoot to disable. Knees, shoulders, limbs—just incapacitate. That pushes you toward the Professional or Hardened tiers and even refreshes your Focus meter. Only headshots when violence’s the only option. Clean jobs matter.

2. Threats > violence

Rob a store, hold people up, scare them—but don’t kill. Rockstar isn’t punishing the hustle, only the mess. Threats cost zero reputation.

3. Play smart in open‐world chaos

Mission-required violence doesn’t ding your profile. But if you're going off-brand—murdering civilians, cops, domestic animals—boom, you’re sliding toward Psycho or worse. Keep it necessary only.

4. Check your profile often

Hit the map and tab over to the Jason & Lucia panel, or hold right on the D‑pad during gameplay. You’ll see your current tier. Helps you course-correct before it cracks irredeemable.

5. Got close to Irredeemable? There's no undo.

Once Irredeemable hits, it won't shift. Rockstar confirmed that’s permanent—so don’t let it get that far. Better to reload a save than live with a shattered rep.

FAQ

Can I lower my Criminal Profile if I’m already Psycho?

Yes—if you haven’t hit Irredeemable yet. Skip unnecessary violence, use non‑lethal moves. But once you’re Irredeemable, the game locks it in.

Does the Criminal Profile affect the story ending?

Rockstar hasn’t confirmed any ending changes. They’ve said consistent actions may tweak the narrative or world’s reaction, but no official word on alternate endings yet.
