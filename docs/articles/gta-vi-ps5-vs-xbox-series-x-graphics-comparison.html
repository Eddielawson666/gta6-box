GTA VI on PS5 vs Xbox Series X: What's the Difference?

If you're wondering how GTA VI looks on the latest consoles, here's the lowdown. Based on what's been shown, the graphics on both PS5 and Xbox Series X are looking mint. Rockstar's official trailers and demos have teased stunning visuals, but there are nuances depending on your console of choice.

Resolution and Frame Rate

From the trailers, it's clear that Rockstar is pushing for top-tier performance. Both consoles are expected to run GTA VI at 4K resolution, aiming for that sweet 60 FPS. Now, Rockstar hasn't officially confirmed exact specs for each console yet, but looking at GTA V's enhancements, you can bet they're going all in.

Ray Tracing and Lighting

Ray tracing is the real MVP here. Both PS5 and Xbox Series X should shine with dynamic lighting and reflections that make the game world feel alive. Trailers have shown off realistic shadows and reflections, but specific implementations per console haven't been detailed by Rockstar.

Loading Times and Performance

Loading times are a big deal, and both consoles promise lightning-fast SSDs. Rockstar hasn't spilled the beans on load times for GTA VI yet, but if it's anything like the improvements seen with GTA V updates, expect to jump into the action quicker than ever. Exclusive Features?

Right now, no exclusive features have been announced for either console. So if you're trying to decide based on that, hold your horses. Rockstar might surprise us closer to launch, but as of now, it's a level playing field.

What We Haven't Seen Yet

While trailers have hyped up the visuals, Rockstar hasn't detailed every graphical feature. We haven't seen specific side-by-side comparisons or official announcements on differences in graphical fidelity between the PS5 and Xbox Series X. It's all speculation until Rockstar gives the nod.

Closing Thoughts

In the end, whether you choose PS5 or Xbox Series X probably won't be based solely on graphics. Both are going to deliver an epic experience if the teasers are anything to go by. Catch up on other cool features like the hidden cars and bikes or the new weather system.
