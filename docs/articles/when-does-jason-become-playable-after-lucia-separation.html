Short answer

You don’t miss Jason for long. He kicks off Chapter 2 sidelined, but he’s back behind the wheel by the mission Breakfast Run. After that—and the following mission Coffee Break—you can swap freely.

Here’s how it actually plays out

Chapter 1 sets you up with Jason in control almost entirely. Lucia gets out near the end, and you briefly play as her. But free-roaming as Lucia only opens with Chapter 2 missions like What’s in the Bag?, and even then switching is locked.

Jason then spends the early part of Chapter 2 recovering in a motel. He only becomes playable again when you hit the mission Breakfast Run. That mission gets you moving again, and after you complete Coffee Break, the game finally unlocks character switching for good.

How long that takes

  • If you’re rushing the main story: expect around 1.5 to 2 hours of Jason downtime in Chapter 2 before Breakfast Run.
  • Some guides use mission counters: Breakfast Run is the eighth mission in Chapter 2 after “What’s in the Bag?”, with Coffee Break right behind it.
  • Free-switching opens right after Coffee Break. After that, you can hop between Jason and Lucia anytime, unless a mission locks you to one character.

Why your mileage may vary

Not all sources agree on the exact mission name that triggers switching—some say Breakfast Run, others say Coffee Break. Rockstar hasn’t confirmed officially. I’m going with what multiple trusted guides report, but fair warning: your game might tip you slightly earlier or later. Just keep an eye on the prompt when you're near the end of that mission.

Final thoughts

It’s a short separation—just long enough to shift the story and your perspective. And honestly? It’s neat to feel like Lucia gets her own space before you can switch. Makes the eventual Team Duval lineup hit harder.

FAQ

Can you play as Jason right after Lucia’s release?

Not immediately. Lucia becomes playable in some story missions late in Chapter 1, but Jason remains in charge until Chapter 2. You don’t get to play as him again until the mission Breakfast Run.

When does free switching between Jason and Lucia begin?

Free switching opens after the mission Coffee Break in Chapter 2. Before that, you’re locked to whoever the story has you following.

How long is Jason out of commission?

About 1.5 to 2 hours if you’re speed‑running the story. He’s out during early Chapter 2 while Lucia runs things solo.
