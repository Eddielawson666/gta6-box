Quick answer

If you're trying to make sense of that massive 26-minute "Extended Look" from Rockstar, here’s the lowdown: it’s built from in‑game footage on PS5—equal parts gameplay and cinematics, tightly edited. There’s loads shown, but you have to watch it alongside Rockstar’s character/place pages to separate confirmed facts from fan theory.

What does Rockstar actually confirm?

  • They shot the Extended Look entirely in‑game on PlayStation 5, mixing gameplay and cutscenes seamlessly.
  • It dropped on August 27, 2026—premiered on Netflix in the afternoon, then hit YouTube and Rockstar’s site later that evening.
  • It’s not called “Trailer 3.” That’s a media nickname. Rockstar calls it “An Extended Look.”
  • The video gives you about 26 minutes of real in‑world play—movements, combat, missions glimpses, police chases, leisure stuff. It's way more than just a flashy teaser.

Rockstar’s pages even help with names and places—use those to keep your take grounded.

How to watch it right

  • Go straight to Rockstar’s official GTA VI media page or channel. No fan edits, no autoplay spoiling things.
  • Follow the release order if you want story context: Trailer 1 (Dec 4, 2023), Trailer 2 (May 6, 2025), then the Extended Look (Aug 27, 2026), and finally the Launch Trailer (Oct 15, 2026).
  • Use Rockstar’s character/place guides while watching. That’s the only confirmed source for names and background, not what Reddit comments say.

What rockstar hasn’t confirmed

  • PC specs, framerates beyond the PS5 presentation, or mods—no clue yet.
  • Total map size, mission order, or detailed story beats—I’ve heard chatter but Rockstar hasn't spelled them out yet.
