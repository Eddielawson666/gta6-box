Where’s Tommy Vercetti’s Shirt in GTA VI?

Google AdSense

Short answer? You trigger a massive Easter egg, go through a portal, and boom—Tommy’s blue Hawaiian shirt and his bike land on your back. It’s wild, and it’s confirmed.

1. Radio tower gig in Key Lento

This starts at the top of the Badger R&D radio tower in Key Lento, near where Jason kicks off his adventure. You pull the lever, and a little TV pops on with a "Club Malibu" logo. That’s your cue to begin the puzzle trail.

2. Hit the 12 substations in order

Rockstar hasn’t spelled them out officially, but insiders have mapped them. You gotta ride around and flip switches at 12 Leonida Power Line substations in the right order. They spell out the old cheat code “LIFE ISPASSING ME BY” using letters tagged on station walls—so yeah, they’re clever with their references. No fast travel allowed.

3. Reactor 2 portal at T.H. Power Station

Once the 12-button run is done, you get a message: “U‑235 REACTOR2 19‑06.” That’s your sign to head to Reactor 2 at the Turtle Head power plant east of Hamlet—but only between 7 PM and 6 AM in-game. Pull the lever beside the Reactor 2 door, step into the pink crackly portal, and brace yourself.

4. Vice City ’02 VIP trip

You drop into a blocky, PS2-era Malibu Club. Jason turns all retro, you find Kent Paul upstairs—he triggers another portal. Go through that, and you pop back into modern Vice City. But now you’ve got Tommy’s blue Hawaiian shirt and his classic chopper waiting. Nostalgia overload.

What’s confirmed and what’s not

  • It’s definitely a real in-game Easter egg—backed by players and trusted guides.
  • Rockstar hasn’t posted an official step sheet, so some station order details come from the community. Still, the core steps (radio tower start, 12 switches, Reactor 2 portal, Malibu Club reward) are rock-solid.
  • No word yet on variations—like what if you miss a switch or use fast travel. Based on the vibe, this one’s supposed to be a bit of a slog.

Why it’s awesome

This is old‑school Rockstar stuff. A scavenger hunt that sends you back in time, a cheeky nod to Vice City, and a reward that actually makes you feel like a legend. It goes deep, man.

FAQ

Do you need to replay the whole thing if you mess up one switch?

Yeah, it’s a sequence puzzle. Skip one or go out of order, and you’ll have to restart the substation loop.

Can you teleport to Reactor 2 or skip the tower?

Nope. Fast travel seems blocked for this. You’ve gotta do the full circuit, probably so you really earn it.
