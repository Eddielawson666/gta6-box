GTA VI’s Immortal NPC: The Unkillable Guy in Leonida

Quick answer

Yes, there’s a legit unkillable NPC in GTA VI—dubbed the Immortal Man—found at a radio tower in Key Lento, Leonida Keys. Rockstar hasn’t officially detailed him. Everything else is players buzzing, still waiting on confirmation.

The Immortal Man: what’s confirmed

Players found an NPC in Key Lento on a radio tower who doesn’t die—even after multiple sniper shots. He climbs the tower, signals you, then jumps into pursuit. It’s eerie, straight out of a horror flick—a weird Easter egg, maybe. None of it’s confirmed by Rockstar, though. It’s purely player-discovered so far.

What players are saying (but Rockstar _hasn’t confirmed_)

  • Sniper fire doesn’t kill him. He just keeps coming. That’s legit across videos and player reports.
  • Some say parachuting down while he climbs cancels his damage immunity.
  • Other wild claims say knifing him stuns him, even gives a money reward. No one’s verified that reliably.
  • Background chatter: theories about aliens or self‑aware NPC glitches—but all unverified.

So what’s real, mate?

Here’s what’s backed by actual clips and multiple reports:

  • He’s in Key Lento, up a radio tower, wearing a light blue tee and cap.
  • You spot him with a sniper scope; he notices and gives you the finger slash gesture before the chase.
  • Sniper fire doesn’t faze him. At all.
  • Some players say parachuting in mid‑climb removes his immunity—just a report, not verified.

Trash talk aside, there’s zero mention of this guy in any Rockstar trailer, Newswire post, or interview. Rockstar has said nothing. So treat that parachute trick or knife tip as just whispers in the lobby—cool ideas, but not fact.

Why none of it’s official

Rockstar hasn’t talked about immortal NPCs, glitches, or alien stalkers. Their focus is on evolving NPC behavior compared to Red Dead Redemption 2—like noticing when you eavesdrop, full‑body interactions, NPCs reacting dynamically. Nothing about immortal stalkers. So all this Immortal Man stuff is strictly community discovery.

Your takeaway

Want to see the Immortal Man for yourself? Head to Key Lento's radio tower, take that sniper, and see what happens. Enjoy it as a creepy, emergent encounter—for now, the game doesn’t officially acknowledge it.

Rockstar’s confirmed the NPC system evolved from RDR2, with more believable reactions and crowd behavior—but nothing about unkillable stalkers.
