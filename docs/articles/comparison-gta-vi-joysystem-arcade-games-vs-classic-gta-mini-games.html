JoySystem Arcade Games vs Classic GTA Mini‑Games

Quick answer

GTA VI’s got four proper in‑game video games—three on the JoySystem console at home, one on arcade cabinets. That’s a big jump from GTA V’s quick distractions. But don’t expect darts or bowling—they’re gone.

What’s confirmed about the JoySystem games

You get this fictional console—JoySystem—in Jason’s living room. You fire it up with the controller next to your TV.

Three games live there: Tenebrae Lux (arty, puzzle), Street Trash III (zombie shooter), and Time Jackers: Rewind (run‑and‑gun shooter). Rockstar cooked them up with an "internal dev team within the dev team". They’re fleshed out, not throwaway bits.

Then there's Bang For Your Buck, a proper light‑gun arcade hunting game found on cabinets dotted around Leonida—one outside Bargain World in Port Gellhorn is for sure.

Starting all four games once counts toward 100% completion; beating all levels of any one earns the 'Stick Fiddler' trophy.

How this stacks up against GTA V mini‑games

Remember GTA V’s mini‑games? Stuff like Go Go Space Monkey or Let’s Get Ready to Bumble. Cute, but they were simple distractions.

Here? You’re talking full‑blown indie‑style titles with cutscenes, custom menus, unique mechanics. None of that old content returns—Rockstar rebuilt from scratch.

What’s still unclear

  • There’s no sign of darts, bowling or casino games in GTA VI—they didn’t make the cut.
  • We don’t know if JoySystem or cabinets expand post‑launch.
  • Locations of all Bang For Your Buck cabinets aren’t fully mapped yet—only one confirmed.

Final thoughts

This is next‑level. GTA VI doesn’t just add mini‑games—it folds in entire games inside the game. It’s clever, self‑referential, and gives you something that feels real, not just a quick side gig.

FAQ

How many JoySystem games are there and what are they?

There are three games on the JoySystem: Tenebrae Lux, Street Trash III (Dezombifier), and Time Jackers: Rewind.

Where do I play Bang For Your Buck?

On physical arcade cabinets scattered across Leonida—one confirmed spot is outside Bargain World in Port Gellhorn.

Do old GTA V mini‑games return in GTA VI?

Nope. Rockstar built brand new games for GTA VI. The old ones like Go Go Space Monkey didn’t make it.
