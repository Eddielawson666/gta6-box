Short answer

Yeah, there’s a hidden Easter egg that actually lets you grab Tommy Vercetti’s iconic Hawaiian shirt – and his motorcycle – by unlocking a secret portal behind Vice City. It’s wild, complicated, and confirmed by multiple players. Rockstar hasn’t officially admitted it, but it’s real.

How the Easter egg works

First, you gotta hit up the radio tower in Key Lento (Badger R&D). Switch on the TV at the top until it plays that faint retro sound effect from old Vice City. That kicks off the sequence.

Next, there are 12 power substations—markers all over Leonida—where each building has a letter. You’ve gotta visit them in the right order to spell out the old Vice City cheat: “LIFE ISPASSINGMEBY,” and you must do it without fast traveling or switching characters. If you nail it, your phone gets a text saying something like “U‑235 REACTOR2 19‑06.”

That points you to Reactor 2 in the T.H. Power Station east of Hamlet. You gotta go between 7 PM and 6 AM in-game time. Pull a lever next to the reactor and… boom: a pink portal opens.

What happens inside the portal

You step through and now you’re in a low-poly, PS2-era Vice City—inside the Malibu Club. Kent Paul is there, just as you remember him. Go upstairs from behind the bar and trigger that next cut-scene, and you’re back in present day, but Jason’s got Tommy’s blue Hawaiian shirt and a matching old-school motorcycle waiting for you.

Players across GameSpot, DualShockers, GTA Scoop, ViceCityDB and Reddit have tested this—same steps, same result. That’s as confirmed as we can get for now. Rockstar hasn’t said a thing officially, though. So take that with a grain of salt.

Why it matters

  • It’s not just a cosmetic drop—it’s a whole mini time-travel moment to Vice City.
  • This isn’t in any trailer or promo—players disco‑vered it live. No leaks, just hardcore exploring.
  • For longtime Vice City fans, it is a nostalgic reference.

FAQ

Yeah, switching characters or fast traveling resets the puzzle. Stay as Jason from start to finish.
