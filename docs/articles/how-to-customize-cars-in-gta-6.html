How to Customize Cars in GTA 6

In GTA 6, customizing cars is a detailed and exciting process that starts with visiting a mod shop. During an early mission, you’ll be introduced to these shops, like Coastal Auto Care in Key Lento, where you can repair and respray your vehicles.[S1]

Where to Customize

There are around 14 mod shops across the game, each focusing on specific vehicle categories such as muscle cars or tuners.[S1] These shops are marked on the in-game map under the Vehicles → Mod Shops filter, and they can be distinguished by icons like orange cars or blue wrenches.[S1]

Getting Started

Before customizing, make sure any stolen vehicle is registered as a personal vehicle. Repairs are necessary if the vehicle is damaged; fortunately, they're free at mod shops.[S2]

Inside the Mod Shop

Once inside, you’ll find a menu with various sections navigable via controller buttons:

  • Pro Builds – Offers pre-made upgrade kits.
  • Cosmetics – Features options like resprays and customizable liveries.
  • Bodywork – Provides aesthetic parts such as bumpers.
  • Performance – Includes engine upgrades and nitrous.[S2]
  • Wheels – For tire and wheel modifications.
  • Interior – Allows changes to steering wheels, seats, and upholstery.[S2]

All upgrades display their impacts on performance stats, so choose wisely according to your driving needs.[S2]

Payment and Saving

Purchases are completed using the Cross (PS5) or A (Xbox) buttons, and it’s essential to have enough in-game cash deposited in your bank.[S2] It’s also wise to reserve major modifications for vehicles you plan to keep, as story progression can affect early vehicle customization.[S1]

FAQ

Can I customize any vehicle in GTA 6?

Yes, as long as the vehicle is registered as your personal vehicle, you can customize it at a mod shop.

Do I need to repair my car before customizing?

Yes, cars need to be repaired first, which is free at mod shops.
