Rockstar's History with PC Ports

Let's take a little walk down memory lane. Remember when GTA V finally hit PCs? Rockstar did a solid job optimizing it, even if it took a bit longer to land than the console versions. It ran pretty smoothly on mid-range hardware when it launched, which bodes well for GTA VI.

Of course, that doesn't mean they'll mirror the specs exactly. But if you're rocking a decent setup from the last few years, you should have a fighting chance when the details drop.

Comparing to the Past: What to Expect

Here's a little comparison to chew on. GTA V launched on PC back in 2015 with these specs:

  • OS: Windows 7 64-bit
  • Processor: Intel Core 2 Quad CPU Q6600 or AMD Phenom 9850 Quad-Core Processor
  • Memory: 4GB RAM
  • Graphics: NVIDIA 9800 GT 1GB or AMD HD 4870 1GB

Fast forward to today, and you'd expect GTA VI to require a fair bit more. An educated guess? Something like a modern i5 or Ryzen 5, 8GB of RAM, and a GPU along the lines of an NVIDIA GTX 1060. But hey, that's just a guess till Rockstar gives us the hard facts.

When to Expect the PC Specs

Rockstar's known for playing their cards close to the chest. So when can you expect the PC specs? Likely closer to the official PC release announcement. Until then, keep an eye on Rockstar's channels and maybe start saving for a GPU upgrade, just in case.
