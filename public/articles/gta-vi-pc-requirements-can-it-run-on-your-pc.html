Can Your PC Run GTA VI?

So, you're itching to know if your trusty rig can handle GTA VI? Here’s the deal: Rockstar hasn't dropped the official PC specs yet. But stick around, because there are some nuggets of info we've pieced together.

What We Know So Far

Rockstar's been pretty tight-lipped about the PC version. But given how GTA V played out, we can make some educated guesses. Expect a game that's more demanding than its predecessor, considering the leap in graphics and the size of the game world.

Comparing to GTA V's Requirements

If GTA V was any indication, you'll want at least a mid-range setup. Here's a quick glance at what GTA V needed:

  • OS: Windows 7 64-Bit
  • CPU: Intel Core 2 Quad CPU Q6600
  • RAM: 4GB
  • GPU: NVIDIA 9800 GT 1GB

Now, imagine turning these up a notch for GTA VI. More RAM, a better CPU, and definitely a beefier GPU are likely in order.

Rockstar's Approach

Rockstar has a knack for optimizing their games, so while you might need a decent setup, they usually ensure it’s not just for the elite. I'd wager they're working to balance stunning visuals with broad accessibility.

PC vs Console

With GTA VI confirmed for next-gen consoles like the PS5 and Xbox Series X, you'd expect similar performance demands on PC. What About Modding?

One of the beauties of PC is modding, and Rockstar games are no strangers to that. While nothing's confirmed about mod support, it's something to consider when picking your hardware. Mods can push your system beyond the game's base requirements.

Stay Updated

As it stands, keep an eye on Rockstar's official announcements for the final word. In the meantime, you might want to check out what we know about GTA VI Multiplayer, just to get hyped up for what’s coming.
