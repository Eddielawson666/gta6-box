GTA VI Multiplayer: Everything We Know So Far

GTA VI multiplayer. It's the talk of the town, and rightly so. Rockstar's been mum on the details, but a few nuggets have slipped through. Here's what we've got.

From the Trailers

The official trailers have dropped, and while they've mostly focused on the single-player chaos, there's a glimpse of multiplayer action. Expect a sprawling online world, much like GTA V, but bigger, badder, and more chaotic.

What Rockstar's Saying

In interviews, Rockstar's hinted at a "persistent online world". This could mean a return to what we know and love from GTA Online but with some juicy new twists. They're keeping tight-lipped, though, so it's a waiting game.

New Features? Maybe.

So, what about new features? Rockstar hasn't confirmed any specifics. But with tech leaps since GTA V, we might see more players per session, dynamic events, and maybe even some destructible environments. It's all speculation, but hey, a gamer can dream.

Platform Play

Will it be cross-platform? Rockstar hasn't uttered a word, but with the industry trending that way, it's a fair bet to think cross-play might be on the cards. Just don't hold your breath until we hear it from the horse's mouth.

The Big Unknowns

Honestly, there's loads we just don't know. Rockstar's playing it close to their chest. We expect more info as we edge closer to the release. Until then, keep your eyes peeled.
