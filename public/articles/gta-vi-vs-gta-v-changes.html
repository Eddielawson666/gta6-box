GTA VI vs GTA V: What's Different?

Alright, mate, let's get straight to it. GTA VI is shaping up to be a beast compared to its older brother, GTA V. We've only got what Rockstar has officially told us, but there's enough to get excited about. Here's what's confirmed so far.

Bigger and Better Map

The first thing Rockstar dropped was the map size. It’s massive. From what we've seen in the trailer, it's not just bigger in terms of area, but more detailed. Think dense urban landscapes mixed with lush countryside. If you loved exploring Los Santos, wait till you get lost in this new terrain. For those interested in secret spots, check out our guide on hidden locations.

Two Protagonists, Twice the Fun

Remember how GTA V switched things up with three protagonists? This time around, Rockstar’s stepped back to two. The dynamic duo promises a more focused story, with the trailer hinting at some serious character development and interactions. If Rockstar nails the storytelling, you're in for a treat.

Improved AI and NPC Interactions

Rockstar’s improved NPC behavior is a big talking point. They’re smarter, more interactive, and apparently have their own lives. Forget the mindless drones of GTA V; these NPCs are bound to surprise you with how they react. It's like living in a real, breathing world.

Graphics to Drool Over

The graphics? They're next-level, mate. Rockstar showcased some impressive visuals in their gameplay reveal. Lighting, shadows, and textures are seriously polished. If you need a good controller to fully enjoy this experience, I'd stick with the DualSense, grab one here.

Revamped Vehicles and Combat

Vehicle dynamics and combat have been given a boost. The driving looks smoother, and the combat seems more fluid. If you've seen the trailer, you'd notice the attention to detail in vehicle damage and gunplay. Rockstar’s clearly aiming for a more immersive experience.

Multiplayer Evolution

While Rockstar hasn't dished out the full deets on multiplayer, they did mention it's going to be bigger and better. Considering the success of GTA Online, you can expect some exciting new features and events. But, we'll have to wait for more concrete info.
