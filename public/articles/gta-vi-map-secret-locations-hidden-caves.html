What We Know So Far

Alright, let's dive right in. GTA VI is packing a massive map, and Rockstar's been teasing us with hints of secret locations and hidden caves. But just how much of it is confirmed? Here's the lowdown on what Rockstar's officially shown us.

Officially Revealed Map Details

From the snippets in trailers and gameplay footage, we know GTA VI's map is going to be huge. Rockstar mentioned that the game will feature a new state called Leonida. This place is set to bring a mix of dense urban areas and sprawling wilderness. Whether you're cruising through a city or exploring the wilds, there's a lot to uncover.

Now, as of the last trailer, we've seen some intriguing spots that scream "secret." But Rockstar's being cheeky and hasn't confirmed exactly where these are.

Hidden Caves: What's Been Shown

So, do hidden caves exist? Yes, but specifics are slim. During a gameplay showcase, there was a brief scene where a character disappears into what looked like a cave entrance. That’s about all we’ve got for now. Could be part of a mission or just a neat spot to discover. We’ll have to wait and see.

Based on the vibes from previous GTAs, especially V, you'd expect a few hidden gems scattered around. Remember the UFOs and hidden tunnels in GTA V? Yeah, Rockstar loves to tuck away surprises. So, it's safe to say VI will have its share.
