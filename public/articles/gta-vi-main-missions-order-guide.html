The Main Mission Structure: What We Know

Alright, let's dive straight into what Rockstar has laid out for us about GTA VI's main missions. Spoiler alert: there’s not much confirmed! They've been keeping things close to the chest, as you'd expect. But, from everything they've shown in trailers and officially released clips, we're getting a feel for the chaotic brilliance they're crafting.

What we do know? You're back to a multi-protagonist setup, much like GTA V. Rockstar confirmed this in a couple of interviews, saying they want to build on the dynamic of multiple character storylines. So, expect some intertwined missions.

Progression and Mission Order

Rockstar hasn't laid out a mission-by-mission order yet, but, based on previous games, you can expect a mix of linear and branching missions. You'll have the freedom to choose missions based on the character you're playing at the time. It's a bit of a sandbox vibe, where story missions can be tackled at your own pace.

If you're the type to plan your attack, you might want to wait for a full mission list to emerge post-launch. Until then, it's a guessing game, mate.

The New Locale: Leonida

Part of the story progression will likely be tied to the new state of Leonida. Rockstar's dropped some hints about how this new setting will impact mission structure. It's a sprawling urban jungle mixed with some rural hinterland. Think of it as how San Andreas mixed city and countryside.

There’s chatter about more dynamic character interactions influencing mission outcomes. Rockstar hasn't confirmed specifics, but if they follow their past trends, choices might impact mission flow. They love that stuff, right? Adds a layer of depth to the chaos.
