What's the Deal with GTA VI Multiplayer?

Alright, mate, so you've been itching to know about GTA VI's multiplayer scene? Here's what we can say: Rockstar has confirmed they're taking what they built in GTA V and cranking it up a notch. Think bigger, better, and a lot more chaotic.

Confirmed Features

From the trailers and Rockstar's announcements, we know multiplayer is going to be a massive part of GTA VI. There's been talk of an evolving world, where players' actions can affect the entire game world. Sounds ambitious, right? But Rockstar's aiming to make it happen.

What About the Map?

The map is supposed to be even bigger than GTA V's Los Santos. Imagine that! While we haven't seen all the nooks and crannies just yet, it's likely you'll find some epic spots to cause mayhem with your mates.

Gameplay Mechanics

Rockstar's keeping a lot under wraps, but it's expected we'll see some tweaks and improvements on the existing GTA Online formula. If you enjoyed the chaos and camaraderie of heists and free-roam activities in GTA V, you won't be disappointed here.

Will It Be on Next-Gen Platforms?

Yes, Rockstar has confirmed GTA VI is being developed for next-gen consoles. If you're rocking a PS5, you're set. For long sessions I stick with the DualSense, grab one here.

What We Still Don't Know

Now, don't shoot the messenger, but Rockstar hasn't spilled all the beans yet. Stuff like specific multiplayer modes, detailed gameplay mechanics, or even the PC release date is still in the shadows. But if history is anything to go by, expect updates as we get closer to launch.
