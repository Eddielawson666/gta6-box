What's Confirmed About GTA VI Multiplayer?

So, you want the lowdown on GTA VI's multiplayer? Right now, Rockstar hasn't given us the full buffet, but here’s what's on the table. They’ve confirmed that multiplayer is happening, and it’s set to be more ambitious than ever. A recent interview with a Rockstar developer highlighted they'd be expanding on the successes of GTA Online. Expect bigger, more dynamic events and environments.

Map and Environment

One thing that's got everyone buzzing is the setting. The multiplayer map will include the new state of Leonida, as hinted in trailers. This promises a mix of urban chaos and rural escapism. Imagine Los Santos on steroids, but with fresh scenery. If you want to dig deeper into how Leonida works, check this out: GTA VI Leonida.

Features and Gameplay

Rockstar's taking cues from previous games, promising deeper role-playing elements. You know, more than just robbing banks and racing cars. There’s talk of businesses and property management being much more involved this time. But until we see it, this is more speculation than fact.

What's the Deal with Crossplay?

This one's a biggie. Everyone's asking: will there be crossplay? Rockstar hasn't confirmed this yet. But given how much crossplay is becoming the norm, you'd expect them to jump on board. Fingers crossed.

Technical Aspects
